## Full description (≤ 4000)
Mu is the messaging and social app built for providers — shops, restaurants, studios, clinics, creators — and the people they serve.

💬 CHATS FIRST
• A dedicated inbox for your business: answer customers, send photos, reply to messages
• Group chats for your team, suppliers or clubs
• Read receipts, typing indicators and online status
• Voice and video calls, one-to-one or in groups — your phone rings even when Mu is closed
• Message notifications with a preview; tap to jump straight into the chat
• Switch between speaker and earpiece during calls

📣 SHARE WHAT'S NEW
• Short posts with a photo
• Choose who sees each post: everyone, your connections, or only you
• Reactions and comments, with notifications

🔴 GO LIVE
• Stream live as your business — your connections and followers are notified
• Live comments and floating reactions, kept on the post afterwards
• Continue a live from another device in one tap

🤝 CONNECT
• Connect with other providers and grow your network
• Followers for your business, with a shareable profile link
• Run several provider profiles from one account, with admins and editors

🔒 SAFE AND IN YOUR CONTROL
• Block and report, moderated by our team
• Calls and lives are never recorded
• Sign in on up to 3 devices; see and sign out devices any time
• Delete your account and data from Settings

Mu works on phones and on the web at mu.cryptdox.com.
